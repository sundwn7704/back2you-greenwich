FAQ

How long can an item stay on the site?

After 30 days, if an item is still unclaimed, it will be removed and hidden from public view.

Is it free to post a lost or found item?

Yes! Posting on back2you is completely free.

What should I do if I found an ID, passport, or wallet?

For government IDs, passports, or bank cards, it is safest to turn them over to the nearest police station or barangay hall.

What if someone tries to scam me or ask for a ransom?

Never send money or gift cards in exchange for returning your item. Report suspicious messages immediately by contacting us through email, and attach screenshots as evidence.
